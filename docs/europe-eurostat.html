Profile

Eurostat migration and asylum statistics

Every country reports the same tables under an EU regulation. They are counts, not records of individual people.

What it is
Official tables on migrant residents, permits, asylum, naturalisations and returns.
Who produces it
Eurostat, with data from national statistical offices and interior ministries.
Unit
Counts of people, permits, applications and decisions.
Coverage
The EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland. The UK until 2019. Every country of origin.
How often
Every year. Asylum data come out every month.
Migration variables
Country of birth or citizenship, reason for the permit, asylum decision and type of return.
Best for
How many LAC migrants live in each country, and through which legal door they arrive.
What we have built with it
Profiles of LAC diasporas in Europe, with a focus on Mexico. Return statistics for Central America.
Access
Free and open. Cite Eurostat.

Profile

Mexican consular records

Mexico registers its nationals abroad at its consulates. The records cover places where surveys are thin.

What it is
Yearly fact sheets on Mexicans registered at consulates.
Who produces it
Institute for Mexicans Abroad, Government of Mexico.
Coverage
159 countries in 2025. A series for 11 countries since 2018.
Migration variables
Sex, age, occupation, state of origin in Mexico and nationality held.
Best for
Mexicans outside the US.
Be careful
Some years repeat the figures of the year before.

Questions it answers

  • How many people born in each LAC country live in each European country.
  • Through which door they arrive: family, study, work or protection.
  • How many ask for asylum each month, and how their cases end.
  • How many become citizens.

Be careful with

Birth or citizenship

Counts by citizenship drop people who naturalize. Use country of birth to size a diaspora.

A permit is not a person

One person can hold several permits over time. Naturalized citizens hold none.

Countries come and go

More countries report each year. Compare the same set of countries over time.

Gaps

France and Germany do not report residents by country of birth. The UK stops in 2019.

Pairs well with

  • 03 Household surveys: Spain's survey adds work and income for LAC-born residents.
  • 04 Global estimates: UN and modelled estimates fill the gaps for France and Germany.
  • 05 Deportation data: returns from Europe next to removals from the US.

How to cite
