03 Comparable data across destinations

Household surveys: LAC, Spain and the US

Three families of surveys share one definition, a person born in another country. So we can compare migrants and natives the same way in Santiago, Madrid and Miami.

One definition, three families

All three families flag people born in another country. So the same indicators work for migrants and natives. The US surveys add the year of arrival. Spain's survey adds years of residence. The LAC surveys only say whether a migrant has lived in the country five years or more.

Profile

IDB Harmonized Household Surveys

National household surveys from 26 countries, put into one common format by the IDB.

What it is
Labour force and living-standards surveys with the same variables in every country.
Who produces it
National statistical offices. The IDB harmonizes them.
Unit
People in sampled households, with survey weights.
Coverage
26 LAC countries, 1986–2025.
How often
Every year in most countries. Some countries every quarter.
Migration variables
Born in another country. Born in another LAC country. Five years or more in the country.
Best for
Comparing migrants and natives on work, education, income and poverty.
What we have built with it
Regional comparisons of migrants and natives. Country notes on migrant integration.
Access
Each survey year is public or restricted. We share aggregates only.

Profile

Spain: Labour Force Survey

Spain hosts the largest LAC diaspora in Europe. About 4.4 million people born in LAC lived there in 2024.

What it is
Spain's quarterly labour force survey.
Who produces it
National Statistics Institute of Spain (INE). Harmonized with the LAC surveys by the IDB.
Unit
People in sampled households, with survey weights.
Coverage
Spain, 2014 and 2018–2025.
How often
Every quarter.
Migration variables
Country of birth, nationality and years living in Spain.
Best for
Work of LAC-born residents in Spain, with the same definitions as the US data.
What we have built with it
A comparison of LAC migrants in Spain and the US by age and employment.
Access
The 2014 file is public. Country of birth for 2018–2025 comes from a special request to INE.

Profile

United States: American Community Survey and Current Population Survey

What it is
The US household surveys. Page 01 profiles them in full.
Best for
The same indicators, by country of birth, age and year of arrival.

Questions it answers

  • What share of each country's population was born abroad.
  • Whether migrants work more or less than natives, and in which jobs.
  • How migrants compare with natives on education, income and poverty.
  • The same questions for LAC migrants in Spain and the US.

Be careful with

Small samples

Where migrants are few, one survey year has few records. Pool years or report margins of error.

Missing questions

Some surveys do not ask where people were born. Mexico's main income survey is one.

National definitions

Each country defines employment its own way. Part of any gap between countries is definition.

Repeated households

Spain's survey visits the same households for six quarters. Margins of error must reflect that.
