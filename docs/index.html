Five families of sources, one map. This site profiles the datasets behind our migration work, from US census microdata to UN stock estimates and immigration-enforcement records, and shows one or two charts per source so the team can see what each one can answer, what it cannot, and where it breaks.

Also in the ecosystem

Other material we hold but do not profile yet

  • DHS OHSS enforcement tables — removals by region and month, and an IPUMS USA extract used for cross-checks.
  • Mexico SEGOB — irregular-migration events in Mexico, 2016–2025, used for Honduran returns.
  • Returnee registries — Honduras SIAMIR and Guatemala IGM returnee records with IOM and SEGOB series; the Northern Central America returns story.
  • Regularization programmes — a compilation of LAC regularizations and Chile's SERMIG permits, 2000–2023.
  • Meta international migration flows — a public estimate of bilateral flows from Facebook data.
  • IOM Missing Migrants Project — deaths and disappearances along migration routes.
  • IDB Lab airport survey of remittance senders — 35,800 interviews, 2014–2021, a migrant survey rather than a household survey.

How to read this site

Conventions used on every page

  • Every chart has three exits below it: the PNG for slides (light and dark), the CSV behind the chart, and a table view for screen readers and for checking a number.
  • Colors mean the same thing across the site. A series keeps its hue whatever is filtered out; grey is always the residual group.
  • A profile block lists the same fields for every source, so a missing field is itself information.
  • Counts come from the source projects' own pipelines; each extractor checks the headline numbers against the project documentation before a chart is drawn.
  • Nothing here is person-level. The repository holds only the small aggregated tables shown in the charts.

A profile of the data sources used to study migration from Latin America and the Caribbean. Every chart is built from small aggregated tables derived from public or publishable sources; no microdata is published here.
