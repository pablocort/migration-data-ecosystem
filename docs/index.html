Also in use

Other sources we use (not profiled here)

  • US enforcement statistics. Monthly government tables on removals and border encounters.
  • Returnee registries. National records of people returned to Mexico and Northern Central America.
  • Regularization programmes. A record of the regularization programmes in the region.
  • Migration flows from social media. Estimates of moves between countries based on social media users.
  • IOM Missing Migrants Project. Deaths and disappearances on migration routes.
  • Survey of remittance senders. Interviews with migrants who send money home, 2014–2021.
