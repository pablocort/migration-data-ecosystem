04 Global anchors

UN DESA and global estimates

Five products give the global picture. The UN counts migrants every five years. A model fills the years in between. UNHCR covers displacement. Population and remittance data complete it.

Profile

UN DESA International Migrant Stock

The UN estimate of how many migrants live in each country, and where they come from.

What it is
Official estimates of migrants by country of residence, origin and sex.
Who produces it
UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division.
Unit
Migrants living in a country at mid-year.
Coverage
About 230 countries and areas, 1990–2024.
How often
Every five years from 1990 to 2020, then 2024.
Migration variables
Country of birth or citizenship, origin and sex. Age only until 2020.
Best for
The official size of each diaspora and of each country's migrant population.
What we have built with it
Regional briefs on migrant stocks. Country tables by origin and destination.
Access
Free and open. Cite UN DESA.

Profile

Annual migration estimates

A statistical model turns the UN figures into a yearly series for every pair of countries.

What it is
Annual estimates of migrants and moves between every pair of countries.
Who produces it
Azose and Raftery (2019), published with Gaskin and Abel (2025).
Unit
Modelled migrants on 1 January, with uncertainty ranges.
Coverage
Every country, 1990–2024.
How often
Every year.
Migration variables
Country of birth, country of residence and moves by previous residence.
Best for
Year-by-year trends, and where each diaspora lives.
What we have built with it
Studies of migration inside the region. Remittances per migrant.
Access
Free and open. Cite the authors.

Profile

UNHCR Refugee Data Finder

People forced to leave their homes, by country of origin and country of asylum.

What it is
Counts of refugees, asylum-seekers and other displaced people.
Who produces it
UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR).
Unit
People at the end of each year.
Coverage
Every country, 1990–2024.
How often
Every year.
Migration variables
Country of origin, country of asylum and type of protection.
Best for
The size of forced displacement from and within the region.
What we have built with it
Displacement tables for LAC countries as origin and as host.
Access
Free and open. Cite UNHCR.

Profile

Remittances

Money that migrants send home, from three sources.

What it is
Yearly totals, flows by sending country, and the cost of sending money.
Who produces it
World Bank and KNOMAD. Central banks of the region. World Bank price survey.
Unit
US dollars and share of GDP.
Coverage
LAC countries, 2000–2025. Flows by sending country for 15 countries.
How often
Every year. Some central banks publish every month.
Best for
How much each country receives, from where, and at what cost.
What we have built with it
Remittances per migrant. The link between diaspora ageing and remittances.
Access
Free and open. Cite each publisher.

Profile

UN population data

What it is
UN World Population Prospects: population by age and sex, with projections.
Who produces it
UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division.
Best for
Denominators: migrants as a share of the population, and ageing.

Questions it answers

  • How many migrants live in each LAC country, and where each diaspora lives.
  • Which migration routes grew in a given year.
  • How large forced displacement is, by origin and host.
  • How much money comes back, from where, and per migrant.

Be careful with

Different dates

UN figures are for mid-year. The annual estimates are for 1 January. Fast-growing groups differ for that reason.

Modelled, not counted

Use the annual estimates for trends. Quote the UN for official levels.

LAC is not the IDB region

The UN region has 33 countries plus territories. The 26 IDB borrowing countries hold 95% of its migrants.

Displaced Venezuelans

UNHCR counts most displaced Venezuelans as other people in need of protection, not as refugees.

Pairs well with

  • 01 United States: survey counts for the US route.
  • 02 Europe: for people born in Mexico, Eurostat and the UN agree within 3%.
  • 05 Deportation data: removals and returns on the US side.

UNHCR Refugee Data Finder. World Bank and KNOMAD, remittances data. UN DESA, World Population Prospects 2024.
