Deportation Data Project: ICE and USCIS records

Records of US immigration enforcement, released through public records requests. Each row is one event, such as a removal, an arrest, a detention or an application.

Profile

ICE removals, arrests and detentions

Records of people removed, arrested or detained by US Immigration and Customs Enforcement.

What it is
One row per removal, arrest or detention, with an anonymous person number.
Who produces it
US Immigration and Customs Enforcement. Published by the Deportation Data Project.
Unit
Events, not people. One person can appear several times.
Coverage
The United States, October 2011 to March 2026.
How often
Daily records, used by month and by fiscal year.
Migration variables
Citizenship, country of birth, entry date, removal country, criminal history and arresting agency.
Best for
Who is removed, how many, and to which country.
What we have built with it
Quarterly reports on removals to the region. Profiles of people removed in 2024 and 2025.
Access
Free and public. Cite the Deportation Data Project.

Profile

TPS and DACA applications

Every application to two US protection programs since 2000.

What it is
Applications for Temporary Protected Status (TPS) and Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA).
Who produces it
US Citizenship and Immigration Services. Published by the Deportation Data Project.
Unit
Applications, not people. Renewals repeat.
Coverage
2000 to February 2026. DACA starts in 2012.
How often
Daily records, used by month.
Migration variables
Country of birth, sex, age group, first application or renewal, and decision.
Best for
How many people apply, how fast cases are decided, and how many wait.
What we have built with it
Monthly series of TPS and DACA. An estimate of Haitians with TPS.
Access
Free and public. Cite the Deportation Data Project.

Profile

Border Patrol encounters

What it is
One row per encounter at the US border.
Coverage
2016–2018 and 2023–2024. The years in between are missing.
Best for
Arrivals at the border by citizenship, in the years we have.

Questions it answers

  • How many citizens of each LAC country the US removes each month.
  • Who is removed: age, sex, time in the US and criminal history.
  • How many are sent to a country that is not their own.
  • How many people wait for a TPS or DACA decision.

Be careful with

Fiscal years

A US fiscal year runs from October to September. FY2025 began in October 2024.

Partial year

Records stop in March 2026. The low FY2026 bar is not a drop.

Expulsions are not removals

Pandemic-era expulsions in 2020–2022 are not in these records. Those years look low for that reason.

Last entry, not arrival

The entry date is the last recorded entry. It does not show how long someone lived in the US.

How to cite

Deportation Data Project (deportationdata.org): ICE removals, arrests and detentions; USCIS TPS and DACA applications; Border Patrol encounters. Records obtained under the Freedom of Information Act.
